# Charging failures in consumer electronics returns

## Typical signs
- The device or its case does not charge, charges intermittently, or stops charging after a short time.
- Customers mention a charging port, cable, case, contacts, or "stopped pairing" together with charging problems.
- Return reason is recorded as "Charging failure".

## Likely causes
- Poorly soldered or loose charging contacts and ports (a hardware quality issue).
- A defective charging cable or adapter bundled with the product.
- A weak charging circuit that fails under normal use.

## What to check
- Is the failure tied to one component (port, contacts, cable) or to the whole product?
- Do charging failures and battery issues appear together? If so, a shared hardware cause is likely.
- Did the failure appear after days or weeks of use (wear) or immediately (manufacturing defect)?

## Recommended actions
- Inspect the charging components and review quality checks for this production batch.
- Test returned units to find the failing part and report it to the manufacturer.
- Replace bundled cables or adapters if they are the cause.
- Add a charging test to outgoing quality inspection.
