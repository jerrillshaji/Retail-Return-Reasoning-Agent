# Compatibility issues

## Typical signs
- Customers report that the product does not work with their phone, operating system, charger or other device.
- Return reason is recorded as "Compatibility issue".
- Feedback mentions pairing problems, unsupported devices or missing adapters.

## Likely causes
- Unclear compatibility information on the product page.
- Software or firmware versions that do not support some devices.
- Missing accessories or adapters that customers assumed were included.

## What to check
- Which devices or versions do the complaints mention most?
- Is a compatibility list published on the listing, and is it accurate?
- Are the complaints about pairing or connection (possibly a firmware bug) or about physical fit?

## Recommended actions
- Publish a clear compatibility list on the product page.
- Release a firmware or software update if a bug is confirmed.
- Provide or recommend the right adapter, and mention it in the listing.
