# Products damaged on arrival

## Typical signs
- Customers report broken, dented or scratched products, or damaged packaging, on delivery.
- Return reason is recorded as "Damaged on arrival".
- Damage reports concentrate on certain carriers, regions or shipping periods.

## Likely causes
- Insufficient protective packaging.
- Rough handling by a carrier or in a particular warehouse.
- Heavy or fragile items shipped in packaging meant for lighter goods.

## What to check
- Are damage claims linked to one carrier, route or fulfilment centre?
- Is the damage the same kind each time (for example crushed corners)?
- Is the product rated as fragile and packed accordingly?

## Recommended actions
- Improve packaging (stronger boxes, inserts, fragile labelling).
- Raise the issue with the carrier or switch carriers for affected routes.
- Photograph damaged returns to document the pattern.
- File carrier claims where applicable.
