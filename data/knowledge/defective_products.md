# Defective product returns

## Typical signs
- Customers report the product stopped working, never worked, or has a part that fails (for example one earbud not responding).
- Return reason is recorded as "Defective product".
- Several customers describe the same fault in their feedback.

## Likely causes
- A manufacturing defect in a specific batch.
- Weak quality control at the factory or at the warehouse.
- A design weakness that fails under normal use.

## What to check
- Do the defective returns share a batch, supplier, date range or warehouse?
- Is the same fault described repeatedly? Repeated identical faults point to a systematic cause, not random defects.
- Do the defect reasons overlap with other reasons such as battery or charging problems?

## Recommended actions
- Review quality-control records and inspect returned units to find the root cause.
- Quarantine remaining stock from the affected batch and inform the supplier.
- Offer replacements promptly to protect customer satisfaction.
- Track the defect rate per batch after the fix to confirm it improved.
