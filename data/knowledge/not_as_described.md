# Products "not as described"

## Typical signs
- Customers say the product differs from the listing: size, colour, features, performance or quality.
- Return reason is recorded as "Not as described".
- Feedback uses phrases like "advertised", "expected", "smaller than shown" or "different from the picture".

## Likely causes
- Product title, photos or specifications that overstate the product.
- Missing information (dimensions, battery life, included accessories).
- Differences between product versions that were not updated in the listing.

## What to check
- Compare the listing claims with the real product specifications.
- Look for the same expectation gap repeated across customer comments.
- Check whether the listing was changed recently or the product revised.

## Recommended actions
- Correct the product page: accurate specifications, realistic claims, clear photos.
- Add missing details such as dimensions, battery life and what is in the box.
- Update customer-service answers to set the right expectations.
